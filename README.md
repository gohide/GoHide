## What it does

Press one global hotkey and the windows, taskbar buttons and tray icons you chose disappear — nothing flashes on screen, nothing lingers in Alt-Tab, no icon left behind. Press it again and everything comes back exactly where it was: same position, same size, same stacking order. Built for focus, screen-sharing, presentations and privacy.

Most "window hiders" leave a tray icon or a taskbar button behind. gohide removes every trace you choose — and restores it cleanly — even on the current Windows 11 notification area, where older tools quietly stopped working. [How tray hiding works on Windows 11 →](https://gohide.net/en/docs/tray-hiding-win11?utm_source=readme)

## Features

- **Hide windows & taskbar buttons** — remove an app's windows and its taskbar button in one move; nothing lingers in Alt-Tab.
- **Hide system-tray icons** — the part most tools get wrong. Works on current Windows 11 builds.
- **Global hotkeys** — one hotkey hides everything at once, one restores everything; per-app hide/show hotkeys and a lock-screen hotkey too.
- **Restore in place** — everything returns exactly where it was; nothing gets stranded.
- **Mute or suspend while hidden** — silence a hidden app, or pause its process so a background game stops using CPU/GPU (suspend is Pro).
- **Privacy cover screen** (Pro) — the moment you hide everything, a cover image or a cover-story app drops over the desktop.
- **Hot corners & mouse triggers** (Pro) — hide all by flicking the mouse into a corner or pressing a side button.
- **Dynamic rules** (Pro) — auto-hide apps by window title or class instead of picking each one.
- **PIN lock** (Pro) — nobody at your desk can change your rules or reveal hidden apps.
- **Auto-hide when you step away** (Pro) — everything hides after 1–15 minutes idle.
- **Hide chosen apps at Windows startup** (Pro) — tray icons included.
- **Start with Windows, elevated** — via a Task Scheduler task, no UAC prompt on every boot.
- **Six languages** — English, 简体中文, Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 한국어; follows your Windows language or keeps the one you pick.
- **Light and private** — no account, no telemetry, works fully offline.

## When another tool fits better

- You want **every** window, desktop icon and the wallpaper gone at once → a whole-desktop boss key such as Boss Key (MindGems).
- You only want a tidier notification area long-term → Windows' own **Settings → Personalization → Taskbar → Other system tray icons** is enough.
- You need apps hidden from **screen-recording or remote-monitoring software** → gohide can't do that; it hides apps on your local screen only.
- You're not on Windows → gohide is Windows-only.
